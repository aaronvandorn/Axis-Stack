What each axis drives

Every effect you add to an axis is stacked: moving that axis sweeps all of them at once, each through its own range and curve. Audio runs through the chains in the order shown.

      Discobot

      Discobot guest

      Axis Stack can sit in Discobot's rack as a guest instrument. In Discobot choose Project → Add Guest Instrument and paste this page's address. Discobot then starts and stops the sample loop on its beat, sets the tempo, takes the sound into its mixer and keeps these settings with each scene. Where Discobot shares its voices, switch Source on the Pad page to Discobot and pick a synth or drum: the pad then plays that voice through the axis chains instead of a sample.
